# Superstore Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses Superstore sales data using SQL and Microsoft Power BI to identify sales trends, product performance, customer contributions, regional performance, and shipping patterns.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products and sub-categories.
- Understand customer segment contributions.
- Analyse regional sales performance.
- Examine shipping methods and average order-to-ship duration.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Power Query
- SQL
- DAX

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary
Provides an overview of total sales, orders, customers, average order value, yearly sales trends, category performance, and regional sales.

### 2. Customer & Product Insights
Examines customer segment contributions, top customers by sales, top products by sales, and sub-category sales.

### 3. Regional & Shipping Analysis
Explores sales across geographical locations, shipping methods, and average order-to-ship duration.

## Key Findings
- The busines generated approximately $2.26 million in sales.
- Sales declined in 2016 but increased considerably between 2017 and 2018.
- The West region generated the highest sales.
- Technology is the leading sales category.
- Consumer customers contribute the largest share of sales.
- Phones and chairs are the strongest sub-categories by sales.
- Sales are concentrated among identifiable high-value customers and products.
- Standard class Ship mode handled the most orders and has the highest average order-to-ship duration.
- New York City contributed the most sales.

## Recommendations
- Monitor average order value over time.
- Investigate which product categories and customer segments contribute to higher-value orders.
- Explore opportunities to increase basket value through relevant product bundles and cross-selling.
- Investigate what contributed to the recovery in 2017.
- Identify the products, regions and customer segments responsible for the growth.
- Examine whether the positive trend continued across individual months rather than relying only on annual totals.
- Investigate the factors behind the strong performance in West and East.
- Examine state and city performance within the South region to identify locations with growth opportunities.
- Investigate whether the product mix differs across regions before making regional expansion decisions.
- Examine which Technology sub-categories drive its strong performance.
- Maintain suitable availability of high-demand Technology products.
- Investigate opportunities to improve sales in Furniture and Office Supplies.
- Prioritise customer engagement strategies for the Consumer segment.
- Investigate which products are most popular among Corporate and Home Office customers.
- Develop targeted campaigns for segments with growth opportunities.
- Avoid neglecting smaller segments, as they still contribute meaningful sales.
- Investigate the products driving sales within Phones and Chairs.
- Ensure the business maintains appropriate stock levels for high-demand products, subject to actual inventory data.
- Explore cross-selling opportunities involving complementary products.
- Investigate whether weaker sub-categories have declining sales or simply lower overall demand.
- Monitor the purchasing patterns of high-value customers.
- Develop appropriate retention and relationship-management strategies.
- Investigate the characteristics of top-performing products to understand what drives their sales.
- Track these customers and products over time to see whether their contributions remain consistent.

## Data Limitations
The dataset does not contain profit, quantity, discount, returns, inventory, or actual delivery date information. Therefore, the analysis focuses on sales and order-to-ship duration.
